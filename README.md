# Moray Tours — Five-page website

Built to the approved proposal scope: five responsive pages, tour and transfer content, gallery, testimonials, enquiry form and basic on-page SEO.

Pages: Home, Tours & Trips (including equipment hire), Transfers & Transport (including local transport), About & Gallery (including vehicle and reviews), Contact.

## Launch checklist
- Confirm all prices, service descriptions, availability and testimonials with Andrew.
- Replace illustrative imagery and recreated SVG mark with licensed original photography and approved vector logo.

- Obtain approved privacy notice and booking terms; the contact page currently contains clearly marked placeholders.
